Meet the orca.

Same idea as the owl in the footer: a small ASCII animal that sits next to the copyright line and reacts to what you do. This one is drawn live, character by character, from a bending spine — so it actually swims.

How it behaves

In light mode it’s drowsy — eye shut, slow tail strokes, the odd bubble from its blowhole. In dark mode it’s wide awake, blinking and swimming harder. Switching theme makes it spout.

Things to try

  • Hover itGets excited, swims fast and spouts.
  • Click itBreaches — curls out of the water and lands with a splash.
  • ScrollPitches with the scroll and flicks its tail.
  • Click a nav linkDives under and resurfaces.
  • Switch themeSpouts, then settles into the new mood.
  • Leave it aloneAfter a few seconds it drifts back to idle.
